# User research profile — Pedro Saffi

## Core themes

These are the substantive areas where Pedro publishes most. When picking a connection-to-your-work hook, prefer concrete overlaps with one of these:

- **Securities lending and equity lending markets.** Lendable inventory, fees, recall risk, beneficial-owner heterogeneity (mutual funds, pensions, insurers). How lending affects price discovery, voting, and shareholder-creditor conflicts. Key papers: *Ownership Structure, Limits to Arbitrage and Stock Returns* (RFS 2016), *Institutional Investors and Voting* (JF 2016), *Securities Lending and Information Acquisition* (R&R Review of Finance), *Shareholder-Creditor Conflicts* (R&R Management Science).
- **Short selling.** Regulation, constraints, price efficiency, diversification puzzle, signed flows, informed shorting. Key papers: *Price Efficiency and Short Selling* (RFS 2011), *Short Sales Constraints and the Diversification Puzzle* (MS 2021), *A Survey of Short Selling Regulations* (RAPS 2024), *The Big Short: Short Selling and House Prices* (REE 2020).
- **Limits to arbitrage.** Ownership concentration, deleveraging risk, funding constraints, regulatory frictions, equity-lending wedges. Key papers: *Deleveraging Risk* (JFQA 2017), *Ownership Structure, Limits to Arbitrage…* (RFS 2016).
- **CDS and the credit–equity link.** How short-selling supply/demand and lending markets affect CDS spreads and the substitution between equity shorting and credit protection. Current R&R at JFE: *Equity Lending, CDS Spreads, and Short Sales Constraints* (with Ghitti, Lopez de Silanes, Matta) — uses NAIC 2010 SSAP 91R insurer reporting requirement and TD9599 dividend tax rule for identification.
- **Hedge-fund activism and short sellers.** Strategic interactions between activists and short interest. Key paper: *Power Tussle: Hedge Fund Activists and Short Sellers* (R&R JFQA).
- **ETFs.** ETF creation, launches, and market implications. Key paper: *ETF Launch Decisions* (R&R JBF).
- **Product complexity and investor sophistication.** How investor experience interacts with the design of complex financial products. Key paper: *Product Complexity, Investor Experience, and Returns* (with de Genaro, Liberti, Sturgess, 2025 WP).
- **Price informativeness, manipulation, and corporate decisions.** Buybacks, manipulation, real investment. Key paper: *Price Informativeness and Corporate Investment* (with Campello, Matta, MS forthcoming).
- **Mutual fund performance and "soft" factors.** Employee well-being as a fund-return signal. Key paper: *Satisfied Employees, Satisfied Investors* (with Ohneberg, 2023 WP).

## Methods toolkit

- Panel data with firm-day, firm-month, or firm-quarter structure
- Instrumental variables driven by regulatory variation (NAIC 2010 SSAP 91R, TD9599 dividend tax rule, short-sale bans, uptick rule, Reg SHO)
- Difference-in-differences (canonical and continuous-treatment)
- Event studies around regulatory or structural-break dates
- Bunching and threshold designs for regulatory cutoffs
- Inference with few clusters; randomization inference where applicable
- Text/measurement work where appropriate (employee well-being scores, complexity indices)

## How to write the "connection" line

- **Pick the most concrete overlap.** Don't say "both work on finance." Say "Both use regulatory variation for identification (you: NAIC 2010; them: Spain LTU threshold)."
- **If overlap is methodological** (e.g., they're an econometrician or a structural modeler), note which of Pedro's designs their work could improve or sharpen.
- **If there's no direct overlap,** find a *conversation hook* — a substantive question their work raises that touches one of Pedro's themes obliquely (e.g., labor-finance angle of short-selling bans, household-portfolio implications of lending revenue).
- **Always actionable** — write a sentence Pedro could literally say at the start of the meeting.
- **Lean on the breadth.** Pedro publishes across securities lending, short selling, ETFs, activism, manipulation, real estate, and product complexity — almost any seminar attendee has some plausible bridge to at least one of these.
